# Portfolio-landlord database ("find the Tims")

The highest-leverage part of this project. Goal: a ranked list of **landlords /
small property managers who own or manage multiple HOUSE rentals** on the target
streets, so Rachel can reach out directly — the strategy she already knows works
(her old landlord Tim).

## Method (how I build & grow this list)

A portfolio landlord leaves the same fingerprint across many listings:
- the **same phone number** on 4+ house listings
- the **same email / contact name**
- the **same small PM company** name on multiple houses (not a big apartment REIT)
- repeated **stylistic tells** in listing copy / photo watermarks

Each sweep I scan current *and* expired listings on the 9 streets, extract every
contact, and cluster. Recurring contact across multiple houses = a "Tim." I record
them below. I will **never invent a phone number or name** — blank = not yet found,
and many will need a human to open a login-walled listing (Kijiji/FB) to read the
contact, which I'll then log here.
