# Outreach templates

Copy-paste, then fill the `[brackets]`. Keep it short, warm, specific, and make
them sound like the easy, low-risk tenants every small landlord wants. Per your
call, these are text only — nobody's inbox is touched until you say so.

> Why these work: small/portfolio landlords screen for *reliable and low-drama*
> over *highest bid*. Lead with stability (two professionals/students, references,
> the 77 Robert track record), and make replying effortless.

---

## 1. To a portfolio landlord / small PM (cold, no specific listing)
> Subject: Two reliable tenants looking for a 2-bed in Harbord Village / the Annex
>
> Hi [Name], I got your name as someone who manages houses around the Annex /
> Harbord Village. My friend and I (two [professionals/grad students],
> non-smokers[, no pets / one cat]) are looking for a **2-bedroom house or upper/main-floor
> unit** in that area, budget up to $3,500/mo, ideally moving in [date].
> I previously rented at **77 Robert St and loved the neighbourhood**. We have
> references, stable income, and can provide whatever you need.
> Do you have anything available now or coming up? Happy to view anytime.
> Thanks! — [Name], [phone]

## 2. Replying to a specific listing (Kijiji / FB / Craigslist)
> Hi! We're really interested in [address / "your 2-bed on Brunswick"]. My friend
> and I are two [professionals/students], non-smokers, and we're looking to move
> [date]. It's exactly what we want — I used to live on Robert St in the area and
> loved it. We can view this week at your convenience and have references + proof
> of income ready. Is it still available? Thanks! — [Name], [phone]

## 3. To Tim (warm — Rachel's old landlord)
> Hi Tim! It's [Rachel] — I rented [your place at __] a while back. [Friend] and I
> are looking for a 2-bed house in the Annex / Harbord Village area (up to $3,500,
> moving [date]). Do you have anything coming up, or know any other landlords in
> the area who might? Would love to rent from you again. Thanks!

## 4. Referral ask (to any landlord who has nothing right now)
> Totally understand! If you happen to know other landlords with houses around
> Harbord Village / the Annex, we'd be grateful for an intro — we're easy,
> reliable tenants and ready to move quickly. Thanks either way!

---

## Tips
- Send #1 to several small landlords/PMs at once; it's a numbers game.
- Always close with a question ("Is it still available?") so replying is one tap.
- Have ready as a single PDF/packet: IDs, references, proof of income/employment,
  and (if available) a one-line reference from a previous landlord. Offer it
  upfront — it makes you the obvious pick.
